Mortgage Broker Bendigo Glossary Explained

Approval language can sound interchangeable, yet each term changes what a lender has actually promised. Conditional approval, often called pre-approval, is the early signal that a borrower may fit policy once documents and the property are checked. The published guide treats it as usually valid for about 90 days, depending on the lender.

Formal approval is the later stage where the lender has assessed the application, the security and the required evidence. Settlement is the end point where the loan is used for the purchase or refinance. For planning, allow around four to six weeks from application to settlement, with lender and borrower circumstances shifting the result.

  • Ask what evidence is needed before the lender sees the file.
  • Ask what would change the recommended lender or product.
  • Time inspections and negotiations so a contract can sit inside the approval window.

Money terms that change lender choice

Loan to value ratio describes the loan amount compared with the property value. It matters because deposit size shapes lender choice, any lenders mortgage insurance question and approval confidence. Lenders mortgage insurance is the cost question that can arise where the deposit is smaller, so it should be raised early rather than after a shortlist is formed.

Deposit is the money already available. Equity is the value built in an existing property that may be used for renovation funding, investment purchase or debt consolidation. Refinancers should state whether the goal is a better rate, consolidation, equity access or investment funding, because each goal tests a different part of the file.

  • Work out available funds before shortlisting products.
  • Separate genuine savings from money still being finalised.
  • Ask how equity affects the recommended structure.

Repayment features in plain English

Principal and interest means repayments reduce the loan balance while also covering interest. Interest only means repayments cover interest for a set period, which can suit some investment modelling but changes later costs and risk. Fixed rate gives a set repayment period, while variable rate can move with market and lender decisions.

An offset account links savings to the loan balance so interest may be calculated on a lower net amount. Negative gearing is an investor term where property costs can exceed rental income, with tax treatment depending on personal circumstances and current rules. The practical point is not the label. The practical point is how each feature is modelled against income, existing commitments, timing and the property goal.

  • Ask when fixed, variable, offset and interest only features are relevant.
  • Model repayments under more than one rate assumption.
  • Keep a buffer for costs that sit outside the loan.

Government scheme terms for first buyers

First home buyers should separate federal and state language. The Victorian First Home Owner Grant is described as applying to eligible new home purchases, with current thresholds checked through the State Revenue Office of Victoria. Eligibility is not assumed; it is confirmed against the current rules and property details.

The First Home Guarantee is administered by Housing Australia. The published guide says it may allow eligible first home buyers to purchase with as little as a 5 percent deposit without lenders mortgage insurance, subject to price caps reviewed annually. Treat that as a possible pathway, not a promise, and check the current cap before setting a search range.

  • Confirm whether the property is new, established or off the plan.
  • Check price caps before making an offer.
  • Ask how scheme eligibility affects deposit, insurance and timing.

Timing, fees and protection words

Broker commission is commonly paid by the lender, while any borrower fee must be disclosed upfront. Best interests duty sits at the centre of the conversation: Australian mortgage brokers must act in the consumer's best interests and prioritise the consumer when providing credit assistance. Credit licence or credit representative status comes from the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 framework, and dispute resolution should be named through the Australian Financial Complaints Authority.

MFAA or FBAA accreditation is a common professional signal, but the sharper questions remain practical. Who holds the licence or representative appointment, how the recommendation is recorded, what fee applies, and when commission disclosure appears should be clear before commitment. Common questions

How long is pre-approval usually useful for? The guide frames conditional approval as typically lasting around 90 days, depending on the lender. Use that window for inspections, negotiations and document checks, rather than treating it as a guarantee that final approval will follow.

What is the difference between a broker fee and lender commission? Most residential brokers are paid by lender commission, while any borrower fee should be disclosed upfront. Ask when the disclosure will appear, who receives each payment and how the recommendation is recorded.

Which terms matter most for an investor file? Investor conversations usually test principal and interest, interest only, offset accounts, fixed and variable rates, existing equity and the proposed structure. The lender will assess current commitments alongside the new property, so the modelling should be explicit.
